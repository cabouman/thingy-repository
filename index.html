🚀 Submit your thingy

  1. Make a free account at huggingface.co.
  2. Create a Space (New → Space), choose the Static SDK and the Blank template, and set it to Public.
  3. Pick an open-source license for your Space — any OSI-approved license you like (Charlie uses BSD 3-Clause).
  4. Upload your thingy as index.html (plus any files it needs). It must run entirely in the browser.
  5. Add a square icon.png (about 400×400) to your Space — that image becomes your tile here.
  6. Give your Space a short description — it shows under your tile.
  7. Test your Space by opening it, then email the link to Charlie using the button below.
✉ Email your submission

Submissions are reviewed before they appear. A thingy is accepted when it runs, is appropriate for a public site, is open source, and has its icon and description. Approved thingys show up on this page automatically.
